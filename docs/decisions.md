| App 1.1.2 (12 Nov 2026): Mere Saboot saves on the phone at once (encrypted) and uploads in the background ("Waiting to upload" until done); the place is looked up with short time limits (last known position, ≤ 4 s fix, ≤ 3 s name); files are encrypted in one pass. | Full-day location is opt-in with its own consent (`2026-11`), needs "Allow all the time", pauses at low battery (`daily.battery_pause_pct`), keeps 7/30/90 days (user's choice), is visible only to the user (admins see counts); adaptive interval from activity recognition | Sensitive data: clear consent, short retention, battery care |

| Community posts are checked by an admin before they show; text with phone numbers or emails is refused; 3 user flags send a post back to review; admins can edit, blur/remove the photo, reject or remove with a reason; grievance contact via Help & Support | IT Rules: moderation and a grievance route |
| Rakshak applications keep only the last 4 characters of the ID; personal details encrypted on the server; 18+; documents deleted on withdrawal and 90 days after rejection | Never store full Aadhaar numbers |
| Plans: Basic / Silver / Gold (2 / 10 / 50 GB vault); **everything free for now** (`everythingFree`, biggest storage for all); payments only through Google Play Billing, checked on the server, refunds/cancellations through Pub/Sub RTDN | Owner's choice; Play policy for digital goods |
